How this estimate works

Returns compound monthly at your annual rate divided by 12. Contributions arrive at the end of each month. The rate and contribution stay constant throughout the projection. Total contributions include your initial investment.

A negative rate models a steady monthly decline. An annual input of −100% means −8.33% per month, not a complete loss in one year.

Projections are illustrative estimates, not guarantees or financial advice. Actual returns vary. Taxes, fees, and inflation are not included.
